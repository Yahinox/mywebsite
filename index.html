Legal Tech Demo

Legal Tech Demo is a simple experimental website for testing legal document review, contract summarization, and compliance workflow automation.

What it does

The platform helps users review contracts, summarize legal documents, compare clauses, and organize important obligations into structured reports.

Features

  • Contract review and risk detection
  • Legal document summarization
  • Clause comparison
  • Compliance checklist generation
  • Obligation tracking

Purpose

This demo page is currently in development and is used to test how public web pages are read, summarized, and indexed by automated systems.
